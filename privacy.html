Surround Map

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Rara Ventures LLC

The short version: Surround Map does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. There is no account. Room scans, measurements and recordings are processed on your iPhone and stay there.

1. Who we are

Surround Map is made by Rara Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. Questions: raraventuresllc@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server for the app and we do not receive your room data, recordings, measurements, device identifier or usage history. The app has no login, no user profile, no advertising and no in-app purchase.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking. The app is built without an active analytics service; if that changes in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first.

3. Information processed on your iPhone

The app processes the following on the device only:

  • Room scan. Camera images and LiDAR depth data are used by Apple's RoomPlan framework to build a 3D room model. The model is saved in the app's private storage.
  • Microphone recordings. Short recordings of test sounds are analysed to estimate each speaker's distance and level. Recordings are discarded after analysis.
  • Your setup. The room model, speaker placement, measurement result and fix list are saved locally so you can reopen them. You can remove them by deleting the room in the app or deleting the app.
  • Local network. When you choose to use a supported streaming box, the app uses your local network to find the player and ask it to start a test file. Nothing is sent to the internet.

4. Permissions

Camera and LiDAR: required to scan the room. Microphone: required to measure speakers. Local network: optional, only for starting test sounds on a supported player. You can change each permission in iOS Settings. If a permission is off, the related step cannot run, but the rest of the app still works.

5. Sharing

We do not share your information because we do not receive it. The app does not include social sharing, cloud sync or a backup service.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete the room in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local storage, subject to normal iOS backup behaviour if you have enabled device backups.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
