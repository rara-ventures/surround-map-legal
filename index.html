Put every speaker in the right place.

Scan your room with LiDAR, see where the soundbar, rears and subwoofer belong, measure each speaker with your iPhone, and get the exact remote settings to fix what is off.

Four guided steps

1. Scan

Map walls, ceiling, sofa and TV with the iPhone's LiDAR sensor.

2. Place

See a spot for every speaker, fitted around the main seat and the places you can actually use.

3. Measure

Play test sounds and let the iPhone microphone check each speaker.

4. Fix

Follow plain-language moves and level changes, then measure again to see the difference.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no tracking. Room scans and recordings are analysed on the iPhone and are never uploaded. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Requirements

iPhone with LiDAR (Pro models) running iOS 17 or later. The first supported soundbar is the Hisense AX700 5.1.4; other gear is coming in later releases.
