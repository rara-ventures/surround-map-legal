Common questions

Which iPhones can scan a room?

Room scanning uses the LiDAR sensor found on iPhone Pro models. On other iPhones the app explains that Step 1 cannot run. iOS 17 or later is required.

The scan keeps failing or the room looks wrong

Move slowly, keep the walls and floor in view, and scan in normal light. Include the whole sofa and the TV wall. If a door or window is missed, you can retake the scan; a saved room is only replaced when you save the new one.

No test sound plays

Check that the iPhone and the player are on the same Wi-Fi network. If your player is not supported, the app gives you a file to play yourself. Make sure the soundbar is on the correct input and the volume is audible.

The measurement says a speaker is silent

Check the speaker's power, its wireless pairing and its cable. Then run the measurement again. The app only reports what it heard from the main seat.

Why does the app ask for microphone or local network access?

The microphone measures each speaker. Local network access is only used to start test sounds on a supported streaming box. Both are optional; the rest of the app works without them. Nothing is uploaded.

Does the app work with my soundbar?

The first supported setup is the Hisense AX700 5.1.4. The app can still help you place speakers with other gear, but the remote-level instructions are written for the AX700. More models are planned.

How do I delete my data?

Open the room in the app and delete it, or delete the app from your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy. See the privacy policy.
